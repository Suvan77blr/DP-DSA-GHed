-> Intro to DP :-

* Dp - a technique for efficient solving of Optimization problems.

* Large Problem -> Smaller Overlapping Subproblems.
* Each sub-prob is solved only once.
* Results of sub-probs are stored to avoid redundant computations.

* Key Steps in DP :-

	1) Id the subprobs and their Rships and understand how the optimal soln can be constructed from them.

	2) Define the recursive rship.

	3) Id the overlapping subprobs.

	4) Choose a suitable approach b/w Memo or Tabu.
		
		Memo ~ Recursive way of solving & storing the results of subprobs in a memo-table. => Top-down approach.
	
		Tabu - Iterative solving & filling up a table / array from the smaller to the larger subproblem.

	5) Applying the chosen approach. => handling the base cases, dfining the necessary data structures & the main logic to solve the problem.

	6) Analysing the TC and SC.


-> Advs of DP :-

* Guarantees Optimal solution by systematically considering all the solutions and then selecting the best one.
* Efficient.
* Use of caching computed results & avoiding recomputation.
* Problem Simplification.
* Can be applied to a wide range of domains.

-> Dis-advs of DP :-

* Needs overlapping subproblems & this is quite challenging to id them.
* Needs problems to have optimal substructure => an optimal solution to the problem must be achievable by the solns of its subproblems.
* May have high computational complexity.
* Designing appropriate recursive rship and formulating the problem as a seq of subproblems can be challenging.
* Tabu can consume substantial memory.
